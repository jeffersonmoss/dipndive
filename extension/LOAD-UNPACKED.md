# Load the Dip'n'Dive extension (unpacked)

Do this on each machine (Thing 1, Thing 2, iMac, the Windows PC). Chrome 116 or newer.

1. Get the code: `git clone` this repo (or `git pull` to update). Do not load it from the Google Drive folder.
2. Open `chrome://extensions`, switch on **Developer mode** (top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the `extension` folder inside the clone (the folder that contains `manifest.json`).
4. The extension ID must be `eokhjchjblklfkhcklpichdfjmmpcana` on every machine (the manifest carries a fixed key). If it differs, the key was lost; do not continue.
5. Open the extension's **Details**, then **Extension options** (later, the small gear in the panel's second row opens the same Settings page). Enter the companion URL (`https://jeffersons-imac.tailb5e03c.ts.net:8443` (port 8443; 443 on the iMac is the gbrain hub)) and the token (copy it by hand from the iMac, `~/Library/Application Support/DipDive/token`). Click **Save and test**.
6. Right-click any link, or the page, and choose **Dip'n'Dive**. The side panel opens. The row has five equal squares, left to right: Continue in Claude, Claude Dip, the page pin, Codex Dip and Continue in ChatGPT. The two Dip buttons change the provider; every summary is a Dip, and "diving" happens in the question box ("Dive in..."); the menu runs the last mode chosen. The center pin summarizes the page in the tab you are looking at (see the note below). The outer arrows are enabled after a Dip finishes; they open the saved Claude or ChatGPT project and hand over the source link, result and fetched transcript/article text.
7. Voice questions (one time per machine): in the panel, click **Enable microphone** (it shows above the question box until the microphone is allowed). A new tab opens and Chrome asks to use the microphone: click **Allow while visiting the site** (not "Allow this time", which forgets the answer when the tab closes). The tab says it worked; close it and go back to the panel. After that the mic button next to the question box works: click once to talk, click again to send. If Chrome says the microphone is blocked, the tab has a button that opens Chrome's setting for the extension; set Microphone to Allow. If the tab or the panel says the computer is blocking the microphone, Chrome is allowed but the operating system is not: on a Mac, open System Settings, Privacy and Security, Microphone, turn on Google Chrome, then quit and reopen Chrome; on Windows, open Settings, Privacy and security, Microphone, turn on Microphone access and "Let desktop apps access your microphone".

Notes:
- Until the companion runs behind `tailscale serve`, the only reachable address is `http://127.0.0.1:PORT` on the iMac itself. Saving that address makes Chrome ask once for permission to reach it.
- Host access is optional: saving the options page asks Chrome for permission to reach exactly that one address.
- The pin (tooltip "Summarize this page"): the first time you click it, Chrome asks to let Dip'n'Dive read your tabs' addresses (the optional "tabs" permission, shown as reading your browsing history). Choose Allow; it asks only once, reads only the page you are on, and you can take it back on the extension's card at `chrome://extensions`. On a browser page such as the new tab page or `chrome://` it says the page cannot be summarized.
- The hand-off arrows ask for optional access to `claude.ai` and `chatgpt.com` on click. They inject a one-time composer filler only in the selected project tab. Claude is left filled and focused for you to review and send; ChatGPT sends automatically when its Send button becomes enabled. If access is declined or the composer cannot be filled, the hand-off is copied to the clipboard.
- Creator line (version 1.2): under the title, the YouTube channel name (a link to the channel's Videos page) or an article's site name (plain text). It is hidden when the companion does not know the creator.
- YouTube history rows: the summary card ends with two rows. For a YouTube video (not in Incognito) the first has the YouTube logo, **History:**, a **Watched** checkbox and thumbs-up and thumbs-down buttons; the second has the Dip'n'Dive icon, **Logged:**, **Summary saved** (see below) and, at the far right, the status text while a Dip runs and the Settings gear. The first tick or thumb click asks for optional `https://www.youtube.com/*` access.
- Thumbs work like YouTube's own: outlined when off, filled when on, click again to undo, and liking turns a dislike off and the other way round. Each click is done in a hidden, minimized, muted window that closes by itself; if it fails the button goes back and the reason is its tooltip and appears under the buttons.
- Watched: tick it and the label counts down "Watch (30s)" (untick to cancel; nothing opens yet). Then it plays the whole video in a hidden, minimized, muted window at the fastest speed YouTube offers and the lowest quality, one video at a time ("Watch (queued)" while it waits, "Watching" while it plays), then it shows "Watched", greyed and locked, and the transcript goes into the monthly transcripts file. Unticking while it plays stops it and shows "Watched (37%)" (your number), greyed and locked, with nothing saved. No Premium is needed and an ad that appears does not stop it. If Chrome will not start playback in the hidden window within 20 seconds, the watch is closed and the box goes back to unticked with the reason. Leaving the video or closing the panel does not stop a watch; closing Chrome cuts it off ("Watched (N%)") and drops the queue.
- Settings has one YouTube section with **Save transcripts of videos I watch** (on by default; it asks once for the tabs permission). There is no Premium, speed or default-action setting any more, and the old clipboard feature and its "This computer is" setting are gone.
- Each video's Watched state and thumbs are remembered on this machine (the newest 500 videos); a new video starts with Watched unticked, both thumbs off and Summary saved ticked.
- The two Dip buttons show the Claude starburst and the Codex logo at full strength. All five buttons are equal squares, black in dark mode and white in light mode (the pin and the arrows have a black outline). A Dive mode stored by an older install becomes the matching Dip.
- The bottom row is History (hourglass), the question box, Speak (microphone) and Ask (return arrow). History opens the whole history log in its own tab (reused if it is already open), with a search box at the top.
- Saving to history: every Dip summary is added to the history log on the iMac when it finishes. In the second row at the end of the summary, **Summary saved** is ticked; untick it (the text becomes **Summary deleted**) to take that summary out of the log, tick it again to put it back.
- Clear history is on the Settings page (the gear), under History, and asks for confirmation. It makes the iMac forget every summary, fetched source and question thread and resets every open panel; the history log file is kept.
- Questions: type in the box under the result and press Enter or Ask (or use the mic). The pressed button decides who answers (Claude or Codex). The panel forgets the conversation when you switch to another link, confirm Clear history (on any machine) or close the panel. If another machine cleared the history while this panel was open, the next button press or question is refused with "History was cleared on another machine.", the panel resets, and you press again.
- To update: `git pull`, then click the reload arrow on the extension card. The question box also needs the updated companion on the iMac: `git pull` there and run `deploy/install-imac.sh` again (it restarts the LaunchAgent).
